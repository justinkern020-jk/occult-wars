battle-choir.ogg / battle-choir.mp3 — intensity layer over the match bed
  Original synthesis (numpy, scripts/gen_battle_choir.py), 2026-10-03, CC0.
  Low formant choir ("ah"/"oh"), deep detuned strings with tremolo and a slow
  funeral-march drum in D minor (Dm, Bb, Gm, A), 39 s seamless loop with a
  circular cathedral reverb. Web Audio fades it in when a match tips (near
  Domination, late rites, a great unit falls). Toggle: Settings > Swelling choir.
